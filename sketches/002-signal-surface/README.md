## Variant: Signal surface

### Design stance
A modern tactical surface where activity is expressed as signal, not decoration or HUD chrome.

### Key choices
- Layout: wide motion field with a dedicated dark progress zone
- Typography: mixed-case Instrument Sans
- Color: deep black, mineral gray, pale chartreuse, muted cobalt
- Motion: three independently phased signal ribbons

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Marathon energy without retro framing
- Weak at: least literal preview placeholder

### Best for
- A more expressive device that still reads cleanly at arm’s length
