## Variant: Quiet field

### Design stance
A calm, preview-led instrument that recedes until something changes.

### Key choices
- Layout: broad render field, compact progress dial, aligned telemetry rails
- Typography: mixed-case Instrument Sans
- Color: warm black with chartreuse and muted cobalt measurements
- Motion: slow atmospheric field and single soft sweep

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: arm’s-length clarity, low visual fatigue
- Weak at: less overtly expressive

### Best for
- A device that remains visible on the desk all day
